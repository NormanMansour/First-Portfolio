About

I am a communication and electronics engineer and junior front-end developer.

I have a deep passion for visual design, with a meticulous eye for the finest details, from correcting a one-pixel misalignment and fine-tuning animation timing to ensuring perfect color consistency. To me, front-end development is an art form: it is about breathing life into static designs with absolute precision, ensuring that the final website looks and functions exactly as envisioned.

Drawing on my academic background in engineering and extensive training in modern web technologies, I focus on building clean, responsive, and highly interactive user interfaces. I also find great satisfaction in transforming complex design concepts into seamless, exceptional digital experiences.
